Portfolio Planning Document

1. Project Overview
Provide a high-level description of your portfolio site.
Goals: I strive to have a showcase where I can lead my future employer/clients to see all of the projects I have done, with fun animation, and intuitive scrolling/finding content mechanism of a webpage. In my portfolio, I plan to make the content to be condensed, and present the animation and clever content organizing for easy access.
Objectives: I will highlight my skills in a validated website first of all, fully functional with good navigation, easy to understand content organization. Furthermore, I want to show off my projects/case studies which will prove to be more descriptive and practical for the future employer to examine first hand my products.
